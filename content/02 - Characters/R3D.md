# R3D

![[02 - Characters/assets/R3D.jpg]]

**PC** — Jessica

## Notes

Rock and roll superstar / front-person singer. Jessica described R3D as a cross between **P!nk** and **Fabian Seacaster**. Use `R3D` as the vault spelling unless corrected.

R3D came from the English generation-ship background and attended a psionic community. Her old best friend/rival [[Callisto]] became her enemy after framing her for embezzling boating-club funds. R3D was the boating club treasurer. [[The Todd]] arrested her after being paid by Callisto.

R3D chose prison rather than simply losing a benefit roll, became a Prisoner/Fixer, hired [[Saul Goodman]] as her lawyer, got out after one term, and came out with much stronger “slapping” skills and more street credibility.

R3D kept contact with [[Saul Goodman]] after prison. Her parents and larger family are not yet defined.

R3D has about **Cr9,000** in personal debt from psionic community/training and legal help during character creation.
