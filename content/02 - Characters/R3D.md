## Notes

Rock and roll superstar / front-person singer. Lefty described R3D as a cross between **P!nk** and **Fabian Seacaster**. Use `R3D` as the vault spelling unless corrected.

R3D came from the English generation-ship background and attended a psionic community. Her old best friend/rival [[Callisto]] became her enemy after framing her for embezzling boating-club funds. R3D was the boating club treasurer. [[The Todd]] arrested her after being paid by Callisto.

R3D chose prison rather than simply losing a benefit roll, became a Prisoner/Fixer, hired a strong lawyer, got out after one term, and came out with much stronger “slapping” skills and more street credibility.

## Relationships

- [[Ritchie Steel]] — knows R3D from a previous band, **Thunderfunk**.
- [[The Todd]] — arrested R3D and put her behind bars.
- [[Callisto]] — former best friend/rival from psionic community; now enemy; framed R3D for boating-club embezzlement.
