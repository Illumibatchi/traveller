## Notes

Aslan guitarist. Ritchie is a cast-out Aslan whose tough, brutal rock persona masks a calmer, shy person who mainly wants to make music.

His Aslan background implies deep shame/exile from clan society. Eric described him as someone who loved music and things opposite to what his society expected, becoming a total failure by Aslan standards and getting sent far away.

Ritchie joined the [[../03 - Places & Factions/Universal Siblinghood of Disunity|Universal Siblinghood of Disunity]] during character creation. He did not become a leading figure; table summary was “not a cult leader, but a cult member — BUNCHA GUYS.”
