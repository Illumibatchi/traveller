## Notes

Callisto was R3D’s former best friend/rival from the psionic community, using a Xena/Callisto vibe. She framed [[R3D]] for embezzling boating-club funds while R3D was treasurer, resulting in R3D going to prison.

Later in character creation, R3D chose to upgrade Callisto from rival to enemy after another betrayal/life-event result. Callisto also paid [[The Todd]] to arrest R3D and is tied to The Todd’s girlfriend leaving him.

## Relationships

- [[R3D]] — former best friend/rival, now enemy; framed R3D for boating-club embezzlement.
- [[The Todd]] — paid him to arrest R3D; stole or moved in with his girlfriend.
