## Notes

Callisto was [[02 - Characters/R3D|R3D]]’s former best friend/rival from the psionic community, using a Xena/Callisto vibe. She framed [[R3D]] for embezzling boating-club funds while [[02 - Characters/R3D|R3D]] was treasurer, resulting in [[02 - Characters/R3D|R3D]] going to prison.

Later in character creation, [[02 - Characters/R3D|R3D]] chose to upgrade Callisto from rival to enemy after another betrayal/life-event result. Callisto also paid [[The Todd]] to arrest [[02 - Characters/R3D|R3D]] and is tied to [[02 - Characters/The Todd|The Todd]]’s girlfriend leaving him.
