## Notes

Ezben is the band's fast-talking manager/accounting person after the previous manager stole the band's money. He leans into opportunities even when they may not be possible, believes in the band with manic self-confidence and enthusiasm, and handles the books because nobody else wants to.

On [[../03 - Places & Factions/Zuflucht|Zuflucht]], Ezben is also the booking/promoter contact previously miscaptured as “Tobias Schultz.” Use **Ezben Schmit** as the correct name unless Cai later corrects the spelling.

He ran after [[R3D]] after her busking performance, threw up, and was walked back to [[../06 - Ships & Gear/Proud Mary|Proud Mary]] to talk with the band. He booked [[../04 - Lore/Generation Jump|Generation Jump]] for a show connected to Larry's / the Factory Plaza scene. Terms captured: **Cr10,000**, free beer, and **2% from bar and booking show**. If the show went well, he suggested a follow-up opportunity at the Vandenzbrugge Rainbow.
