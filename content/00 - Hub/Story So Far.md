# Story So Far

> Campaign recap linking the major setup facts, relationships, and current story state. Updated through **Session 001**.

## Session 001 - Band, debt, and the Infinity Suite

The Thursday Traveller campaign began on 2026-09-03 with character creation and a campaign briefing. The campaign is about a rock band and its entourage touring through isolated space near the Great Rift. The background is grim: dangerous people are pursuing a plan that could lead to war and conquest. The foreground is chaos: rockstars, shenanigans, debt, rivals, reviews, and personal growth.

The band was a small but successful bar-band type act with a couple albums and gathering momentum. Then the previous manager disappeared with the money, leaving the band broke and unable to pay the mortgage on their touring ship, the [[../06 - Ships & Gear/Proud Mary|Proud Mary]]. They begin with **75 million dollars in debt** and **1 Megastar point**.

Their answer is the [[../05 - Rules & Procedures/Infinity Suite Notes|Infinity Suite]]: a seven-movement concept album/suite meant to change the minds of the universe. Their ultimate goal is to successfully perform the full suite in concert and holo-record it.

## The PCs

- [[../02 - Characters/R3D|R3D]] is Jessica/Lefty’s singer/front-person, a P!nk + Fabian Seacaster style rockstar with psionic training, prison history, and an enemy in [[../02 - Characters/Callisto|Callisto]].
- [[../02 - Characters/Ritchie Steel|Ritchie Steel]] is Eric’s Aslan guitarist, a clan-exiled musician whose terrifying stage persona hides a shy person who just wants to make music. He is connected to the [[../03 - Places & Factions/Universal Siblinghood of Disunity|USD]].
- [[../02 - Characters/The Todd|The Todd]] is Alex/Maz’s theremin/electronics character, a former law-enforcement agent whose life veered into music after a theremin epiphany.

Their backstories are already tangled: The Todd arrested R3D after being paid by Callisto; Callisto framed R3D for boating-club embezzlement and later became her enemy; The Todd stole and pawned Ritchie’s sentimental guitar before helping recover it; R3D and Ritchie know each other from the earlier band **Thunderfunk**.

## The band around them

The PCs kept Reina, Kia Ju, Jose Barik, Dimitry Muam, Arkhu “Pyro” Piette, Jonni Siedel, and Ezben Schmit with the band/crew. Rekasen and Izan Guukis were sent away and became the seed of a rival band.

## Current location / region

The first confirmed world is [[../03 - Places & Factions/Zuflucht|Zuflucht]] in the New Islands Subsector. The region sits in/near the Great Rift and was settled by old European Space Agency generation ships long before jump drive spread there. There is no FTL communication, so touring is once again how fame and stories travel between worlds.
