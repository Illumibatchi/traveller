# Story So Far

> Campaign recap linking the major setup facts, relationships, and current story state. Updated through **[[../01 - Sessions/Session 002 - Zuflucht Arrival and Larrys Brawl|Session 002]]**.

## Session 001 - Band, debt, and the Infinity Suite

The Thursday Traveller campaign began on 2026-09-03 with character creation and a campaign briefing. The campaign is about a rock band and its entourage touring through isolated space near the Great Rift. The background is grim: dangerous people are pursuing a plan that could lead to war and conquest. The foreground is chaos: rockstars, shenanigans, debt, rivals, reviews, and personal growth.

The band was a small but successful act with a couple albums and gathering momentum. Then the previous manager disappeared with the money, leaving the band broke and unable to pay the mortgage on their touring ship, the [[../06 - Ships & Gear/Proud Mary|Proud Mary]]. They begin with **75 million credits in debt**, **Cr3.2 million in the safe** as operating budget, and **1 Megastar point**.

Their answer is the [[../05 - Rules & Procedures/Infinity Suite Notes|Infinity Suite]]: a seven-movement concept album/suite meant to change the minds of the universe. Their ultimate goal is to successfully perform the full suite in concert and holo-record it.

## Backstory - New Moscow and Generation Jump

The band formed in [[../03 - Places & Factions/New Moscow|New Moscow]] in Imperial space. [[../02 - Characters/R3D|R3D]] was on the run from [[../02 - Characters/Callisto|Callisto]] and collecting bandmates. [[../02 - Characters/Ritchie Steel|Ritchie]] was down on his luck, stealing cars, gambling, and between bands. [[../02 - Characters/The Todd|The Todd]] arrived broke after spending his own savings and his girlfriend's savings trying to reach the Imperium and survive while practicing theremin.

Todd was involved in stealing Ritchie's guitar and getting it back to him. R3D and Ritchie recognized each other from their earlier band, **Thunderfunk**. The group formed a rock/metal band called [[../04 - Lore/Generation Jump|Generation Jump]].

## The PCs

- [[../02 - Characters/R3D|R3D]] is Jessica’s singer/front-person, a P!nk + Fabian Seacaster style rockstar with psionic training, prison history, a Saul-style lawyer, a large Weasley-like family, and an enemy in [[../02 - Characters/Callisto|Callisto]].
- [[../02 - Characters/Ritchie Steel|Ritchie Steel]] is Eric’s Aslan guitarist, a clan-exiled musician whose terrifying stage persona hides a shy person who just wants to make music. He wants the Infinity Suite to be his guitar masterwork.
- [[../02 - Characters/The Todd|The Todd]] is Alex/Maz’s theremin/electronics character, a former law-enforcement agent whose life veered into music after a theremin epiphany.

## Session 002 - Zuflucht arrival

After eight Jump-1 transits from Imperial space through [[../03 - Places & Factions/Chandler Station|Chandler Station]], Proud Mary arrived at [[../03 - Places & Factions/Zuflucht|Zuflucht]] low on supplies, fuel, and patience. Zuflucht is a neutral, German-speaking, low-law participatory democracy with a tainted thin atmosphere, a Class C starport, and an Imperial Navy presence. The destroyer [[../06 - Ships & Gear/Irmulak|Irmulak]] acknowledged Proud Mary as under Imperial protection.

On the ground at Vandenzbrugge, the band found the music scene. R3D's busking drew [[../02 - Characters/Ezben Schmit|Ezben Schmit]], who booked Generation Jump for Cr10,000 and free beer. A museum visit fed the Infinity Suite: Ritchie learned about [[../06 - Ships & Gear/Von Haalans Guitar|Von Haalan's Guitar]], Todd reflected on leadership, and the group lowered the Infinity Overture target to **11/12**.

The first show at Larry's / Factory Plaza went over well, but collapsed into a dock-worker brawl. Kia Ju, Jonni, Ezben, and R3D were hurt before Todd talked the crowd down. The venue boss still loved them enough to offer **Cr25,000 to rescore _Battleship Potemkin_ (1925)** for a live screening.
