# Session 002 - Zuflucht Arrival and Larry's Brawl

Date: 2026-09-10
Status: parsed from FVTT log

## Summary

After eight weeks and eight Jump-1 transits from Imperial space, [[../06 - Ships & Gear/Proud Mary|Proud Mary]] arrived in the [[../03 - Places & Factions/Zuflucht|Zuflucht]] system. The trip had been cramped and tense because temporary fuel bladders filled much of the cargo bay. Supplies were low, the crew was out of fuel or nearly so, and everyone badly needed shore leave.

Zuflucht proved immediately strange by Imperial standards: a neutral, German-speaking, low-law participatory democracy with a tainted thin atmosphere, an Imperial naval base, and almost no visible customs or law enforcement. The Imperial destroyer [[../06 - Ships & Gear/Irmulak|Irmulak]] acknowledged that Proud Mary was under Imperial protection, whether the band liked it or not.

The band chose a low-key landing at Vandenzbrugge rather than hotdogging. Once planetside, they looked for music opportunities. [[../02 - Characters/The Todd|The Todd]] worked the crowd for leads, [[../02 - Characters/Ritchie Steel|Ritchie Steel]] hunted for open-mic energy, and [[../02 - Characters/R3D|R3D]] busked hard enough to attract a vomiting promoter, [[../02 - Characters/Ezben Schmit|Ezben Schmit]]. Ezben booked **Generation Jump** at Larry's / the Factory Plaza scene for **Cr10,000**, free beer, and a shot at something bigger at the Vandenzbrugge Rainbow.

A museum visit produced mixed inspiration for the [[../05 - Rules & Procedures/Infinity Suite Notes|Infinity Suite]]. R3D tried and failed to turn colonial guilt into a song, Ritchie found a clue about [[../06 - Ships & Gear/Von Haalans Guitar|Von Haalan's Guitar]], and The Todd reflected on leadership and his past as a cop. The group reduced the Infinity Overture requirement by 1, to **11/12**.

The first show at Larry's went well musically, but the aftermath turned into a dock-worker brawl after stage chaos spilled into the crowd. [[../02 - Characters/Kia Ju|Kia Ju]] was badly hurt, [[../02 - Characters/Jonni Siedel|Jonni Siedel]] took a minor wound, [[../02 - Characters/Ezben Schmit|Ezben Schmit]] took a severe wound, and R3D took a major wound and was stunned after an elbow-first stage dive. The Todd ended the fight by appealing to the crowd and buying a round.

Despite the violence, the crowd loved the band. The venue boss offered a new job: **Cr25,000 to rescore _Battleship Potemkin_ (1925)** for a live screening, likely at the Factory Plaza.

## Zuflucht arrival details

- UWP: `C545720-8`; Ag/Pi; Non-Aligned, Human-dominated.
- Atmosphere: thin and tainted; the smell gets everywhere even with filtering.
- Government: participatory democracy.
- Law level: 0, no prohibitions.
- Tech level shown in world data: 8; Jessica noted local repairs may be difficult compared to Proud Mary's higher ship tech.
- Most locals speak German.
- No extrality line, scans, or ID checks at the port.
- Vandenzbrugge is pre-gravitics in layout: dense buildings, surface roads, awkward for grav vehicles.
