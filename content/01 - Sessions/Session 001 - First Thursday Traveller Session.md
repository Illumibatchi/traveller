## Summary

The campaign opened with setup and character creation for a Traveller rock-band campaign. The PCs chose to create their own Travellers rather than play the provided band NPCs. The group’s band is a small but somewhat successful act whose previous manager vanished with the money, leaving them broke and unable to pay the mortgage on their ship, the [[../06 - Ships & Gear/Proud Mary|Proud Mary]]. The band begins with **75 million dollars of debt** and **1 Megastar point**.

The core plan is to create and eventually perform **the [[../05 - Rules & Procedures/Infinity Suite Notes|Infinity Suite]]**, a seven-movement concept album/suite meant to change the minds of the universe. The campaign tone was framed as rockstar shenanigans and personal growth against a grim background of dangerous people pursuing a plan that could lead to war and conquest.

The starting region is the Great Rift / New Islands area. These worlds were originally settled by European Space Agency generation ships before jump drive reached the area, leaving isolated old-Earth enclaves where touring matters because there is no FTL communication.

## Ship: Proud Mary

The band’s ship is the [[../06 - Ships & Gear/Proud Mary|Proud Mary]].

Known facts:

- Based on a subsidized merchant, heavily modified for touring.
- Ship has **Jump-1**.
- Fuel capacity supports **two jumps** under normal circumstances.
- No weapons.
- Deployable stage comes out of the top of the ship.
- Speaker stacks/sound towers emerge from the roof/sides.
- Lined with pyrotechnic mortars.
- Has a recording studio.
- Still has about **100 tons of cargo space** after modifications.
- With the current ten-person band/crew, there is apparently an extra stateroom and no required hot-bunking.

## Traveller / setting facts learned

- No FTL communication: planetary virality exists, but stories spread between worlds by travel.
- A jump takes about **one week**.
- Jump-1 travels one parsec; Jump-2 travels two parsecs; high-end Imperial Navy ships can do more.
- In this sparse region, Jump-1 routing is a real limitation.
- Extra fuel bladders could extend practical reach but would consume cargo space and time.
- The New Islands / Great Rift area was settled by three European Space Agency generation ships built inside hollowed-out asteroids.
- Each ship carried roughly 100,000 people, with about 750 awake at a time, and took about 2,000 years to arrive.
- The Third Imperium contacted the region roughly 200 years ago; jump drive has been spreading since.
- Locals may speak English, German, or French due to generation-ship origins; Galanglic is the broader Imperial/Earth-derived common language.

## Rules and procedures covered

See [[../05 - Rules & Procedures/Traveller Quickstart - Session 001|Traveller Quickstart - Session 001]] for the cleaned-up quickstart.

Session 001 covered:

- Traveller task rolls: 2D6 + characteristic DM + skill DM.
- Difficulty examples: Routine 6, Average 8, Difficult 10, Formidable 14.
- Effect as margin/success output.
- Montage/performance scenes where each PC contributes one skill roll.
- Critics/review rolls using 2D6 plus cumulative montage Effect and local modifiers.
- Megastar points: current score starts at 1; desired long-term rating is over 100.
- Every 10 Effect can create a bonus such as a boon, experience, contact, or other reward.
- Background skills: EDU DM + 3 choices at level 0; level 0 means trained, while untrained rolls take DM-3.
- Luck can be spent before rolling, recovers with downtime, and can be used for random targeting/saving-throw style checks.
- Psionics: PSI, talents, recovery, and dangers.
