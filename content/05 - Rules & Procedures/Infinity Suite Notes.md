## Core idea

The Infinity Suite is the band’s seven-movement concept album/suite — “the concept album of the ages,” with a Wild Stallions meets Pink Floyd vibe. The band wants to change the minds of the universe with it.

## Known win condition

- Perform a concert where the band successfully plays through all seven movements.
- Ultra-win: holo-record the performance.
- If they nail it, their dreams come true and they change the universe.

## How progress works

Known from [[01 - Sessions/Session 001 - First Thursday Traveller Session|Session 001]]:

- Each movement/section has a number of Effect required to play that section.
- Playing parts of the suite in shows can lower those numbers.
- Each movement has events scattered in space that can teach the band about that segment and lower the Effect needed to complete it.
- Hijinks can generate Effect.
- Deep character-building moments with PCs or NPCs can generate more Effect.
- Character growth and better understanding of the universe are core to progressing the suite.

## Related procedures

- Performances use montage/narrative-scene rolls: each person contributes one skill roll.
- Critics/reviews can change Megastar points after a performance.
- Cumulative Effect from the performance helps determine review outcome.
- Every 10 Effect can create a bonus/reward such as a boon, XP, contact, or other benefit.
