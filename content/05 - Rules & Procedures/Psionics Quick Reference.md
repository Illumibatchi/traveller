# Psionics Quick Reference

Type: rules reference
Status: summarized from campaign notes

## Core idea

Psionics are mental abilities such as telepathy, telekinesis, clairvoyance, awareness, and teleportation. They are powerful but unreliable and socially/legal dangerous in many Traveller settings.

## Psionics and the law

- In Charted Space, the Third Imperium bans psionics.
- Psionics Institutes went underground after a disastrous attempt to guide human development.
- Other societies, such as the Zhodani Consulate, accept psionics openly.

## Psionic Strength (PSI)

- PSI powers psionic abilities.
- PSI is normally rare and requires Referee permission during character creation.
- Determine PSI with `2D - number of terms served so far`.
- PSI 0 means no psionic potential.
- Using powers spends PSI points and temporarily reduces PSI.
- Lower current PSI makes further psionic checks harder because PSI DM is based on current PSI.
- If a power cost would reduce PSI below 0, excess becomes damage.

## Recovery

- Expended PSI recovers at 1 point/hour.
- Recovery begins three hours after the Traveller last used a psionic talent.

## Testing and training

- Finding a teacher/underground institute may itself be an adventure.
- Testing usually costs at least Cr5000 and takes two weeks.
- Training costs Cr100000 and takes four months.
- During training, the Traveller attempts to learn talents via PSI checks.
- Each previous talent acquisition check applies DM-1.
- If a talent is learned, it starts at skill level 0.

## Psionic Training table

| Talent | Learning DM |
|---|---:|
| Telepathy | +4 |
| Clairvoyance | +3 |
| Telekinesis | +2 |
| Awareness | +1 |
| Teleportation | +0 |
| Per previous talent acquisition check | -1 |

Telepathy note: if chosen as the first talent, Telepathy is gained automatically.

## Psionic talents

- **Telepathy:** reading minds and communicating.
- **Clairvoyance:** perceiving at a distance.
- **Telekinesis:** mind over matter.
- **Awareness:** control over one’s own mind/body.
- **Teleportation:** instant movement.

## Using powers

- Roll the relevant psionic skill check plus current PSI DM and other DMs.
- Spend the listed PSI cost on success, or 1 PSI on failure.
- Most powers have Reach.
- Range can be increased with higher PSI cost:
  - +1 range band: double PSI cost.
  - +2 range bands: quadruple PSI cost.
