# Traveller Quickstart - Session 001

> Rules and procedures actually discussed during Session 001. Player-facing quickstart, not a full rules dump.

Basis: [[../01 - Sessions/Session 001 - First Thursday Traveller Session|Session 001]] and campaign rules notes

## Basic task roll

When a Traveller does something uncertain and important:

1. Roll **2D6**.
2. Add the relevant **characteristic DM**.
3. Add the relevant **skill DM**.
4. Add any situational DMs.
5. Compare to the target number.

Difficulties mentioned:

| Difficulty | Target |
|---|---:|
| Routine | 6 |
| Average / normal | 8 |
| Difficult | 10 |
| Formidable | 14 |

## Skills

- Skill **0** means trained but no bonus.
- Untrained skill attempts are normally **DM-3**.
- Skill levels commonly range from 0–4 for normal play, with exceptional specialists going higher.
- Characteristic DMs and skill DMs stack.
- Background skills during character creation were **EDU DM + 3** choices at level 0.
- Taking a level in a skill you already have increases it; taking level 1 in an untrained skill can bring it straight to 1.

## Effect

Effect is the margin or useful result generated by a roll/scene.

Session-specific uses:

- Performance montages accumulate Effect from everyone’s contributions.
- Reviews can use cumulative montage Effect.
- Every **10 Effect** gives a bonus/reward of some kind.
- Possible rewards mentioned: short-term boon to a task, experience points, contacts, or other benefits.

## Performance montage

For performances, Cai described the formal term as a **narrative scene**, but preferred thinking of it as a montage.

Procedure as explained:

1. Everyone involved contributes one skill roll.
2. Usually each person uses a different skill, though different instruments/roles can count separately.
3. The group’s cumulative Effect contributes to the performance result.
4. Art critics then review the performance.
5. Megastar points may go up or down based on the review.

## Critics and Megastar points

- Starting Megastar points: **1**.
- Long-term aim: get above **100**.
- Megastar points represent popularity and tour success.
- Higher Megastar rating means fewer things have to be paid for and more people come to concerts.
- Points come from performing well and doing things that create notoriety / go viral locally.
- Because there is no FTL communication, stories do not spread between worlds unless people travel.
- Review rolls use **2D6 + cumulative performance Effect + local modifiers**.
- Researching local critics and what they care about can help by improving review modifiers.

## Money

- The band starts **75 million dollars in debt**.
- The previous manager vanished/stole the money.
- Small rockstar expenses can usually be handwaved through tour funds.
- Big purchases, mortgages, ship debt, and personal debts still matter.
- R3D acquired personal debt from psionic community/training and a lawyer during character creation; exact final debt total should be checked on the sheet.

## Luck

Luck can be used in two ways discussed during character creation:

- Spend Luck before a die roll to improve it.
- Use Luck for saving-throw/random-targeting style rolls, such as determining who gets hit by a bad event or receives a good event.

Luck regenerates during downtime.

## Jump travel

- A jump takes about **one week**.
- Jump-1 means one parsec in a week.
- Jump-2 means two parsecs in a week.
- High-end Imperial Navy ships may reach Jump-4 or Jump-5.
- The [[../06 - Ships & Gear/Proud Mary|Proud Mary]] has Jump-1.
- The ship can carry enough fuel for two jumps under normal circumstances.
- Extra fuel bladders could extend reach but use cargo space and take multiple weeks/jumps.
- Running out of fuel in deep space is a real concern.

## Psionics

See also: [[Psionics Quick Reference]].

### PSI

- PSI powers psionic abilities.
- Current PSI determines the PSI DM used on psionic skill checks.
- Spending PSI makes further use harder until recovered.
- If a power cost drops PSI below 0, excess becomes damage.
- A Traveller with no PSI cannot attempt powers.

### Recovery

- Recover 1 PSI per hour.
- Recovery begins three hours after the last psionic talent use.

### Talents

| Talent | What it does | Learning DM |
|---|---|---:|
| Telepathy | Reading minds / communicating | +4 |
| Clairvoyance | Perceiving at a distance | +3 |
| Telekinesis | Mind over matter | +2 |
| Awareness | Body/Jedi-style self-control | +1 |
| Teleportation | Move through space instantly | +0 |

- Each talent acquisition check after the first has a cumulative DM-1 penalty.
- R3D learned Teleportation, Telekinesis, Clairvoyance, and Telepathy; she did **not** learn Awareness.
- One learned psionic talent was raised to level 1; R3D chose Teleportation.
- Exact final sheet should be checked before relying on all values.

### Teleportation notes from session

- Simplest teleportation is moving yourself a short distance without equipment.
- Teleporting with gear is harder than teleporting without gear.
- A few tens of metres should be easy for R3D.
- Five kilometres is possible but hard for a novice.
- Taking extra time can reduce the difficulty and allow longer-range attempts.
- Claims of planet-to-planet teleportation exist as False Knowledge / rumor.

## Taking extra time

Traveller can reduce task difficulty if a character takes extra time. This came up during teleportation: taking time with a ritual/longer preparation can make a harder task more possible.

## Character creation procedures touched

- The group used a shared rolled characteristic array: everyone contributed rolls, then each player assigned the full array to their own stats.
- Species can modify stats and add traits. Ritchie’s Aslan changes included STR +2, DEX -2, Dewclaw, and Aslan Senses.
- Aslan Senses grant DM+1 on Survival and Recon rolls.
- Pre-career options used included University, Psionic Community, and School of Hard Knocks.
- Careers used included Prisoner/Fixer, Agent/Law Enforcement, and Entertainer/Performer.
- Every term has a survival roll; in modern Traveller, failing usually causes a mishap rather than instant death.
- Terms can generate events, advancement, benefits, contacts, enemies, rivals, promotions, and skills.
- Connections between Travellers grant skill gains and establish backstory.

## False Knowledge

False Knowledge was described as “X-Files shit” — rumors and forbidden/denied weirdness such as space whales, ghost ships, and things official science says cannot exist. Jessica compared it to Pendragon’s Folklore skill.
