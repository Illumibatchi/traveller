# Irmulak

## Notes

The **Irmulak** is an old Imperial Navy destroyer in orbit over [[../03 - Places & Factions/Zuflucht|Zuflucht]]. It is far beyond local construction technology.

On [[../06 - Ships & Gear/Proud Mary|Proud Mary]]'s arrival, Irmulak sent a greeting message acknowledging that Proud Mary was under Imperial protection, apparently whether the crew liked it or not, but did not otherwise interfere with the landing.
