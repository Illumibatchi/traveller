# Von Haalan's Guitar

## Notes

A legendary Old Earth guitar said to have been used by a famous musician. Nobody is quite sure whether the name is **Von** or **Van**, **Haalan** or **Halen**.

The instrument reportedly came to the Islands on one of the generation ships and now sits in a private collection, never played or shown. [[Ritchie Steel]] believes it may be the “past instrument” he needs for the [[../05 - Rules & Procedures/Infinity Suite Notes|Infinity Suite]].

## Campaign hook

The guitar is apparently on the **English ship** / English-descended side of the colony-ship legacy.
