# Proud Mary

![[06 - Ships & Gear/assets/Proud Mary.jpg]]

## Notes

The **Proud Mary** is [[../04 - Lore/Generation Jump|Generation Jump]]'s ship. It is a modified subsidized merchant refit for touring and performance, with a deployable stage, sound towers, pyrotechnics, and a recording studio.

The band owes a huge debt and cannot pay the mortgage on the ship.

## Known specs / features

- Based on a subsidized merchant.
- Jump-1.
- Internal fuel reserves allow two consecutive one-parsec jumps under normal circumstances.
- Temporary fuel bladders can extend practical range but take up cargo space.
- During the trip to Zuflucht, fuel bladders extended range to four parsecs but made the ship cramped and unpleasant.
- No weapons.
- Has a fuel scoop; the band should probably scoop hydrogen when possible.
- Deployable stage comes out of the top of the ship.
- Sound towers / speaker stacks come out of the roof/sides.
- Lined with pyrotechnic mortars.
- Has a recording studio.
- Still has about 100 tons of cargo space under normal load, but much less when fuel bladders are installed.
- May be able to ferry up to 8 people in low berths.

## Current status

- Arrived at [[../03 - Places & Factions/Zuflucht|Zuflucht]] after eight Jump-1 transits from Imperial space.
- Supplies are low and tempers are frayed.
- Ship is nearly/out of fuel.
- Local refuel cost: **Cr8,000**.
- Purging the water recycling system should be a priority after the crossing.
- Local TL8 facilities may struggle with higher-tech ship repairs.

## Travel notes

- A jump takes about one week.
- Jump-1 makes routes in the Great Rift / New Islands area restrictive.
- The route to Zuflucht took four jumps from Cerebin in the Usher subsector to Chandler Station, then four more jumps from Chandler to Zuflucht.
