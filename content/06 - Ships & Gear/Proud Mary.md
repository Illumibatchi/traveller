## Notes

The **Proud Mary** is the band’s ship. It is a modified subsidized merchant refit for touring and performance, with a deployable stage, sound towers, pyrotechnics, and a recording studio.

The band owes a huge debt and cannot pay the mortgage on the ship.

## Known specs / features

- Based on a subsidized merchant.
- Jump-1.
- Fuel for two jumps under normal circumstances.
- No weapons.
- Deployable stage comes out of the top of the ship.
- Sound towers / speaker stacks come out of the roof/sides.
- Lined with pyrotechnic mortars.
- Has a recording studio.
- Still has about 100 tons of cargo space.
- Band/crew totals 10 people after Session 001 decisions; there is apparently one extra stateroom and no required hot-bunking.

## Travel notes

- A jump takes about one week.
- Jump-1 makes routes in the Great Rift / New Islands area restrictive.
- The ship can make two jumps before refuelling under normal circumstances.
- Fuel bladders could extend reach to three jumps but would consume cargo space.
