## Short version

Zuflucht means **haven** in German. It is a neutral, human-dominated world at the far spinward-coreward corner of the Islands cluster. It is friendly, low-law, communal, and strongly shaped by refugee-colony history.

## Colony history

Zuflucht was founded by the colony ship **Voyageur** as a poorly planned seed-colony mission. Voyageur was supposed to scout the system, establish a starter colony, and send word back for a second wave. Instead, large numbers of refugees from [[Topas]] boarded during Topas's civil-war collapse.

The colony was badly overloaded. Early settlements grew around eight fusion power units: six original mission units and two built from Voyageur spares. Voyageur stayed in orbit for 30 years as depot, medical/training center, workshop, and shuttle garage before returning to the central cluster.

Because there were too many people for the available infrastructure, Zuflucht could not maintain its original tech base. It fell back to steam-powered industry for a time, but preserved enough knowledge to recover to TL8.

## Modern society

- Roughly 60 million people spread across the globe.
- Peaceful despite very little formal law.
- Social norms and stigma do much of the work of “law.”
- Serious crimes are handled informally but vigorously by concerned citizens.
- Government administration is handled by professional clerks/bureaucrats.
- Decisions are made by participatory democracy.
- Everyone has a vote, including children; guardians cast votes for minors until adulthood.
- A worldwide datanet keeps citizens informed and supports voting.

## Imperial relationship

Zuflucht is outside the Imperium but protected by treaty. The orbital component of the Class C starport is bigger than usual because it supports a small Imperial naval base. Locals are generally friendly to “The Fleet,” their term for the Imperial Navy.

The world is sometimes treated unofficially as an Imperial Client State, partly so locals can try to join the Navy despite being outside Imperial borders.

## Vandenzbrugge and the port

The downport sprawls along one side of **Vandenzbrugge**. The city looks pre-gravitics: dense buildings, surface roads, and only a few grav vehicles threading awkwardly between structures.

The first things offworlders notice:

- tainted-atmosphere smell, even indoors or aboard ships;
- German as the main local language;
- no extrality line;
- no scans or ID checks;
- very few uniforms or visible law-enforcement structures.
