## Notes

The Universal Siblinghood of Disunity, or **USD**, is not really a religion despite what outsiders may assume. It is a decentralized political-philosophical movement whose members share a loose cluster of values and an “us vs them” worldview.

Their slogan/line:

> “We are legion, each of us standing alone. Disunited we resist the forces of Entropy.”

During Session 001, [[../02 - Characters/Ritchie Steel|Ritchie Steel]] joined or had joined the USD through a political-movement character-creation event. He did not become a leading figure.

## Beliefs

- Members believe everyone and everything is linked by a universal structure.
- They believe information passes along this structure, linking all sentient beings.
- They consider this dangerous.
- They expect or fear an “information apocalypse.”
- They believe people must disunite to stand against entropy.
- Their explanations rely heavily on badly mangled physics, with quality varying by speaker.
- Members often think they possess a truth that outsiders simply “don’t get.”

## Outside view

Most outsiders would summarize them as mostly harmless but annoying nutcases: people who talk nonsense most of the time, occasionally saying something startlingly strange or interesting.
