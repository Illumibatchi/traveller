# Generation Jump

## Notes

**Generation Jump** is the band's name, chosen after the PCs came together in [[../03 - Places & Factions/New Moscow|New Moscow]]. Their style is rock/metal. “Country Western Screamo” was mentioned as a joke/possibility, but the table settled on rock/metal.

The band is trying to develop and eventually perform the full [[../05 - Rules & Procedures/Infinity Suite Notes|Infinity Suite]].
